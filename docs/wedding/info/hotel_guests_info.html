A note from us

Villa days, nearby nights

To keep things relaxed and comfortable for everyone, a few of you will be staying at a lovely nearby B&B. That doesn't mean any less time together — we'd love for you to spend as much time as possible with us at the villa. But if you need a quiet morning, an afternoon nap, or a little peaceful time to yourself between events, take it.

Where you'll be sleeping

Casal Sant'Elena · a country B&B

A beautifully restored farmhouse in the Tuscan countryside near Lucca, run by a lovely Italian family. Quiet, green, with a swimming pool, a big garden, and an unhurried, proper-Italian feel. Easy parking, comfortable rooms, and breakfast on the terrace — exactly the kind of place to recharge between our wilder days.

Good to know

Details about the stay

The things that matter when many of us share one beautiful house, even just for the day. Treat the villa as if it were a friend's home — because that's exactly what it is for these few days.

Pool Days & Sun Moments
  • Bring your pool towel from the B&B — especially on Saturday for the Pool Gathering.
  • Please hydrate and use sunscreen — the Tuscan sun is sneakier than it looks.
  • Please rinse off before jumping in — it keeps the water clean for everyone.
  • Please be careful with glasses around the pool — no bare feet should ever meet a broken glass.
  • Sunglasses, a hat, and a glass of something cold — the official pool dress code.
The Little Crew
  • We just kindly ask that parents keep a gentle eye on their own children while we're all together at the villa.
  • A few spots deserve extra attention — the pool, stairs, glass doors, and busier event evenings — so the kids can explore happily and safely.
  • If we all look out for each other's little ones, everyone gets to relax — many loving eyes are better than few.
Weekend Moments & Champagne Problems
  • We have four gatherings across the weekend — the Saturday Pool Gathering, the Saturday dinner, Sunday lunch and Sunday pizza dinner — with drinks flowing happily from afternoon into the early hours, so please pace yourselves and drink responsibly, especially if you're driving back to the B&B.
  • If something gets damaged or broken, please tell us straight away — never an issue, just easier to sort together.
  • Please don't leave bottles, cups or trash lying around after the events.
Smoking
  • Smoking is only outside, in the designated garden or terrace areas.
  • Never near the pool, the bedrooms, or any soft furnishings.
  • Please use an ashtray and follow the same rules at the B&B.
Looking After the Villa Together
  • Please clean up after yourself in the kitchen, around the pool, in the bathrooms and on the terrace.
  • If you use a glass or a plate, please rinse it or put it in the dishwasher rather than leaving it on a table.
  • Please help us keep the shared spaces calm and tidy by taking personal things with you.
