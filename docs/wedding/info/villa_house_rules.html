Where we are

Relais Corte Benedetto · Capannori, Tuscany

A 19th-century villa set in the green hills of Capannori — 20 minutes from Lucca, an hour from Pisa or Florence. About 870 m² of space, 13 rooms each with its own private en-suite bathroom, an 18-metre pool, a huge open-plan kitchen, and long communal tables made for slow Italian meals.

Bedrooms
13 rooms, all en-suite
Every room has its own private bathroom with shower. We'll assign your room when you arrive — keeps things easy.
Bed
One double bed per room
Each room has a single large double bed. If you're sharing with a partner, it's yours together; if not, you'll have a room to yourself.
Outdoor
18m pool & terrace
A huge pool with sun loungers for everyone, and a long terrace made for sunset dinners over the hills.
Inside
Big open kitchen & lounges
Two living rooms, a dining room with a C-shaped table for 26, and an open-plan kitchen with a central island — perfect for cooking together.
Comforts
A/C, Wi-Fi, heating
Air-con in every room, fast Wi-Fi throughout (network details on the fridge), and heating if the evenings turn cool.
Parking
Space for 6 cars
Private parking inside the gate. Based on who's coming, we're expecting around 8 cars from the villa crowd — which means a couple will have to park slightly further away. We're checking the options nearby and will coordinate on the spot.
Your room: we'll show you to it when you arrive — it'll be a private room with one large double bed and its own en-suite bathroom. We've thought about who's sleeping where, but please be flexible on the day — small swaps sometimes need to happen, and we promise everyone ends up happy.

Good to know

Details about the stay

Everything useful in one place — nothing scary, just the little things that keep a house full of people feeling lovely and relaxed. Treat the villa as if it were your own home.

Gentle House Notes
  • A/C is available in every room — please switch it off when you leave the room or open a window.
  • Turn off lights and appliances when not in use.
  • Kitchen: the fridge is stocked for breakfast and snacks — help yourself. If you finish something, leave us a note so we can replace it.
  • Wi-Fi network & password are on the kitchen fridge.
Keeping Things Lovely & Tidy
  • The villa is not cleaned during our stay — no one comes in to tidy, so let's all pitch in.
  • Please clean up after yourself as you go.
  • If you use dishes or glasses, please leave them in the dishwasher.
  • Keep the shared spaces — living rooms, garden, kitchen — tidy throughout the stay.
Pool Days & Sun Moments
  • The pool is open all day — enjoy it whenever you like.
  • Please hydrate and use sunscreen — the Tuscan sun is sneakier than it looks.
  • Please rinse off before jumping in — it keeps the water clean for everyone.
  • Please bring your towels from your room out to the pool, and take them back after.
  • Please be careful with glasses around the pool — no bare feet should ever meet a broken glass.
The Little Crew
  • We just kindly ask that parents keep a gentle eye on their own children throughout the days.
  • A few spots deserve extra attention — the pool, stairs, glass doors, and busier event evenings — so the kids can explore happily and safely.
  • If we all look out for each other's little ones, everyone gets to relax — many loving eyes are better than few.
Weekend Moments & Champagne Problems
  • We have four gatherings across the weekend — the Saturday Pool Gathering, the Saturday dinner, Sunday lunch and Sunday pizza dinner — with the drinks flowing happily from afternoon through to the early hours, so please pace yourselves, drink responsibly, and enjoy every minute.
  • If something gets damaged, spilled or broken, please tell us immediately so we can handle it together — no judgement.
  • Please don't leave bottles, cups or trash lying around after the events.
Smoking
  • Smoking is only outside, in the garden or on the terrace.
  • Please use an ashtray — never the flowerbeds or the pool.
  • No smoking inside the bedrooms or living areas.
