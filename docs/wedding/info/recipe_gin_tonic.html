Gin
& Tonic

Crisp, cold, simple. The bartender's go-to.

1 serving ~1 min Built in glass
Ingredients
Gin
50 ml
Tonic waterfridge-cold
150 ml
Cucumber slices
— or — rosemary sprig
2–3
Lime wedgeoptional
1
Big ice cubes
to fill
Method
1
Fill a copa glass (or tall highball) completely with big ice cubes.
2
Pour in the gin over the ice.
3
Add the tonic slowly, pouring down the side of the glass to keep the bubbles alive.
4
Drop in cucumber slices or a rosemary sprig. Stir once — gently — and serve.
Glass: copa de balón or tall highball  ·  Tip: 1 part gin to 3 parts tonic is the magic ratio. Cucumber for fresh, rosemary for herby.
